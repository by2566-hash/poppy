- Across equal-length 12-hour windows, NYC recorded 1,455,425 daytime crashes versus 813,762 nighttime crashes, so reported crashes were less frequent at night ([chart](out/option_1.png)).
- At night, 27.9% of reported crashes involved an injury, compared with 22.8% during the day ([chart](out/option_2.png)).
