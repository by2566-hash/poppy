**Yes, crashes dropped dramatically after COVID and have remained far below pre-pandemic levels.**

**Key findings:**
- **Pre-COVID (2017-2019 avg):** ~225,000 crashes/year
- **2020 (COVID onset):** 113,000 crashes (-50%)
- **Post-COVID (2021-2023 avg):** ~104,000 crashes/year (-54% vs pre-COVID)
- **2024-2025:** Further declined to ~85,000-91,000/year

The drop coincided with NYC's March 2020 lockdown (crashes fell from ~17K/month to ~4K in April 2020). While crashes partially recovered in late 2020, they never returned to pre-pandemic levels — stabilizing around 8,000-10,000/month vs 17,000-19,000/month in 2019.
