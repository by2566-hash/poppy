Chart saved to `out/nyc_traffic_fatalities_2016_2025.png`.

**Trend:** Fatalities were flat in the mid-240s through 2019, jumped during the pandemic years (269 in 2020, peaking at 297 in 2021), then fell four straight years to 229 in 2025 — a 23% drop from the peak and the lowest annual total of the decade (2 below the prior low of 231 in 2018).

**Notes:** Sum of `number_of_persons_killed` per crash year; only 36 records in 2016–2025 have a null value (treated as 0), so the totals are unaffected. Per the README, the NYPD data is preliminary and subject to revision.
