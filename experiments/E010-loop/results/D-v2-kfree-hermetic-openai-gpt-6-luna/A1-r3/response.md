The file has complete annual data through 2025, plus a partial 2026, so I’m comparing full years 2013–2025. It supports counts of pedestrian casualties and crash events, but not exposure-adjusted risk (for example, per trip or per resident).
ANSWER:
Annual pedestrian casualties fell 21.7%, from 3,671 in 2013 to 2,874 in 2025, though they have risen since the 2020 low ([chart](out/option_1.png), [values](out/values_1.csv)).
Pedestrian deaths increased from 30 in 2013 to 39 in 2025, a 30% rise ([chart](out/option_2.png), [values](out/values_2.csv)).
Crashes involving at least one pedestrian injury or death fell 20.9%, from 3,498 in 2013 to 2,766 in 2025, after a 2020 low ([chart](out/option_3.png), [values](out/values_3.csv)).
