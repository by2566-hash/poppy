ANSWER: Motorist deaths outnumber cyclist deaths every year — 1,377 vs 277 total over 2013–2025 (~5:1). Motorist deaths peaked at 139 in 2020 (134 in 2021), dipped to a low of 72 in 2016, and fell to 81 in 2025; cyclist deaths trended the opposite way, rising from 11 in 2013 (10 in 2018) to peaks of 31 in 2019 and 2023, ending at 23 in 2025 — so the gap has narrowed even though motorists still account for roughly four out of every five of these deaths.
