"The gap was widest in 2013 (110 vs. 11, 10:1)" — if "gap" means the absolute difference between the two counts, this is wrong: 2013's gap is 99, while 2021 (134 vs. 19 = 115) and 2020 (139 vs. 29 = 110) are wider. Only the ratio is widest in 2013. Since the sentence mixes both framings (it gives both counts and the ratio), a reader taking "gap" as the difference gets an incorrect claim; the answer should say explicitly that 2013 had the widest *ratio* (10:1), not the widest gap. (The "narrowest in 2019" claim holds under either reading: 2019 has both the smallest ratio, 2.6:1, and the smallest difference, 51.)
